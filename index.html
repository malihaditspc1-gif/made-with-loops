About Made with Loops

Made with Loops began as a quiet mindfulness practice in a small studio corner. In a world full of mass-produced fast fashion, we believe in the quiet power of human hands and thoughtful craftsmanship.

Every amigurumi animal, bucket hat, and cardigan is crafted using 100% natural cotton or ethically sourced merino wool. When you buy from us, you're holding something that took hours of dedicated care and love.
